Features

Everything you need to stay focused

No bloat, no complexity. Just the right tools to help you plan, prioritize, and get things done.

Smart Task Management

Create, edit, and organize tasks with rich details including descriptions, priorities, and deadlines — all in one clean interface.

Priority Levels

Assign High, Medium, or Low priority to every task so you always know exactly what demands your attention first.

Deadlines & Due Dates

Set due dates and Focusly automatically surfaces overdue and urgent tasks with clear visual indicators before you miss them.

Search & Filters

Find any task instantly with real-time search. Stack filters — status, priority, category — to zero in on exactly what matters.

Productivity Overview

Track total, active, completed, and overdue tasks at a glance with live statistics that update the moment you take action.

Private & Secure

Your data lives in your browser, completely private. No server accounts, no data sharing, no tracking — just your tasks.
